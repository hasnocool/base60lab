# Core Finding: Frequency Ratios & Harmonics

## Finding Metadata
- **finding_id**: F-43
- **statement**: Base-60 is the ONLY base where all 11 just intonation intervals terminate exactly
- **assessment**: **Good** — Base-60's 2²×3×5 factorization contains all prime factors for JI intervals
- **plain_language_explanation**: Base-60 can represent all 11 standard musical intervals (perfect fifth 3:2, major third 5:4, etc.) exactly as finite sexagesimal decimals. No other base can do this.
- **why_it_matters**: Exact music representation without temperament compromise
- **applications**: Music software, DAWs, tuning systems, music education
- **how_to_implement**: Use `base60lab.frequency.FrequencyRatio`, `Harmonic`
- **what_graphs_show**: JI intervals in cents, interval representations across bases, ET vs JI, harmonic series, phase representations, synthetic signal
- **breakthrough_ideas**: Base-60 Just Intonation Engine for microtonal composition
